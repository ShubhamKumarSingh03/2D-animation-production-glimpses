## 🎬 2D Animation Production Workflow

This project was independently developed and produced from concept to final delivery, covering the complete 2D animation production pipeline.

### 🔹 Pre-Production

**01. Story & Concept**  
Developing the core idea, theme, characters, and overall narrative direction.

**02. Script Writing**  
Writing the complete story, dialogues, scenes, and sequence structure.

**03. Character Development**  
Designing characters, defining their visual identity, appearance, personality, and expressions.

**04. Storyboarding**  
Planning shots, camera angles, character actions, transitions, and scene composition.

**05. Visual & Scene Planning**  
Breaking the story into individual scenes and planning the required assets, backgrounds, characters, and actions.

---

### 🔹 Production

**06. Character & Asset Design**  
Creating the characters, props, and other visual assets required for the animation.

**07. Background Design**  
Designing backgrounds and environments according to the story and scene requirements.

**08. 2D Animation**  
Animating characters, objects, movements, expressions, and scene actions.

**09. Scene Composition**  
Combining characters, backgrounds, props, and other elements into complete scenes.

**10. Camera & Motion**  
Creating camera movements, pans, zooms, transitions, and other visual motion.

---

### 🔹 Post-Production

**11. Voice & Dialogue**  
Integrating character dialogues and voice elements into the scenes.

**12. Sound Design**  
Adding environmental sounds, character sounds, and other audio elements.

**13. Music & SFX**  
Adding background music and sound effects to enhance the storytelling and overall experience.

**14. Compositing**  
Combining animated elements, backgrounds, effects, lighting, and other visual layers into the final shots.

**15. Video Editing**  
Arranging scenes, timing shots, transitions, dialogues, music, and effects into the final sequence.

**16. Color & Visual Polish**  
Refining colors, visual consistency, timing, effects, and overall presentation.

**17. Final Rendering**  
Rendering the completed animation in the required resolution and format.

**18. Final Video Production**  
Reviewing, polishing, and exporting the final animated video for delivery.
